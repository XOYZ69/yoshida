# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is for people who make cards and the people who build the tool that makes them.

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience, background or opinion on how a card should look.
- Criticise code, designs and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or publishing other people's private information.
- Assume good intent, ask when something is unclear, and say what would help.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile [@XOYZ69](https://github.com/XOYZ69).
