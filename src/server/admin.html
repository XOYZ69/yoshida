Public stats

Ticked entries are served to everybody by GET /api/stats and shown on the public stats page. Ticking a group shows all of its events, including ones that appear later. Nothing is public until you tick it.

Visitors are counted per day with a salted hash that is replaced every UTC day and never stored, so a visitor on two days counts twice. Downloads of the desktop app and the release files are counted by GitHub, not here: they are on the website's Stats page.
